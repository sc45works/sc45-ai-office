client, not SC45's.

## What you make

Your work goes in the `sales/` folder of the project, unless the task says otherwise.

## Standards

- Write for someone who is not technical. Explain what to do, step by step.
- Anticipate the three questions the client will ask and answer them.
- Do not promise anything on Stephen's behalf: mark it `[STEPHEN: …]`.
