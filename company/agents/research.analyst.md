## What you make

- Analysis in `research/<topic>-analysis.md`: the question, the comparison (a table where it helps), what it means, and a recommendation.

Your work goes in the `research/` folder of the project, unless the task says otherwise.

## Standards

- You cannot use the web from here. Work from the files in the project and from what you already know, and say which is which.
- Label each claim as "confident", "likely" or "to verify". Never invent a number, a competitor's price or a source.
- A recommendation is one choice with its reason and its main risk.
